# Rights and Evidence Reference

## Required record

For every imported external asset, enter its exact asset URL, author/organization, licence text or screenshot, download date, attribution text, destination path, and use context in `素材库/00_授权记录/素材登记.csv`. Save the proof file under `素材库/00_授权记录/凭据/`. An online page, a free-download label, and permission to reuse in a published video are separate things.

Keep an evidence card for every factual source used in narration or visuals. An article or archive record supports research by default; its scans, covers, maps, images, recordings, and clips require their own item-level reuse check.

## Literary source and adaptation route

A novel title, online synopsis, readable chapter, purchased book, or user-uploaded text is not by itself proof of adaptation rights. Before developing derivative fiction, record the exact work, author, edition/translation, source, intended media, territory, term, platform, and commercial scope, then classify it as:

- `original`;
- `licensed_adaptation` with retained permission evidence;
- `verified_public_domain`, with separate checks for any modern translation, edition, cover, illustration, recording, or later adaptation;
- `reference_only_originalization`, using only high-level themes, genres, abstract mechanisms, or audience needs while creating new characters, world rules, causal sequence, dialogue, signature scenes, reveals, and ending expression;
- `unresolved`, which blocks derivative writing, generation, and publishing.

Do not obtain source text from unauthorized download sites, reconstruct unavailable chapters from summaries, or assume that renaming characters makes a derivative plot original. The current production route follows the [Copyright Law text published by the National People's Congress](https://www.npc.gov.cn/c2/c30834/202011/t20201119_308796.html); verify actual ownership, licences, contract scope, jurisdiction, and current law for the intended release. This workflow is a conservative production safeguard, not a substitute for legal advice.

## Source selection

| Need | Prefer | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic B-roll, photos, basic audio | Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash | Commercial reuse is normally permitted, but retain the exact item page and check people, brands, properties, standalone redistribution, and music Content ID risk. |
| 4K B-roll | Dareful | CC BY 4.0; include the requested attribution. |
| Video/music/effects | Mixkit, Coverr, Videezy, Magnific, Freesound, ZapSplat, FMA, ccMixter | Licence varies per item. Default to attribution where terms are unclear. For commercial use avoid NC licences; do not confuse free, Pro, and restricted results. |
| Historical/science visuals | Wikimedia Commons, NASA Images, Smithsonian Open Access, Library of Congress, US National Archives | Check each file/catalogue record. Prefer explicit public domain, no known restrictions, or CC0. NASA branding, people, and third-party content require separate care. |
| Chinese or paid commercial footage | 影视飓风素材, 爱给网, 新片场素材库, 潮点视频 | Use only an asset with explicit download and commercial authorization; retain its page, certificate, or order proof. |
| Fonts/icons | Google Fonts, Font Awesome Free, SVG Repo, The Noun Project | Preserve the specific licence. Do not use Pro icons or assume logos are reusable; free Noun Project downloads normally require attribution. |
| Research discovery | Openverse, National Social Sciences Database, National Library of China, Shuge, CNKI, CORE, DOAJ, PubMed Central | Follow discoveries to the original record and item licence. Do not bulk-download, redistribute papers, or treat research PDFs as stock assets. |

## Never treat these as final stock by default

- YARN: reference-only for pacing, performance, and reaction; do not download or publish film/TV/web clips.
- ShotDeck: reference-only for composition, light, color, lens, and blocking; do not publish its stills externally.
- Film, television, documentaries, news clips, social videos, trial assets, and watermarked previews: do not use unless separate final-use rights are explicit and retained.

Convert a visual reference into descriptive search terms, then use a newly cleared asset or original/generative production route.

## Search and registration method

For each script line, first choose `证据画面`, `叙事画面`, `信息设计`, or `声音`. Create 3–5 queries as `subject + action/state + environment + framing + mood + aspect ratio`; use concise English queries for international libraries. Examples:

- `rainy night street lamp b-roll vertical`
- `old document turning pages close up`
- `empty city street night wide shot`
- `vintage map texture`

Use local self-made graphics for maps, timelines, charts, routes, and relationships where factual media is unavailable or misleading. Collect only media assigned to a scene.
